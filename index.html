      How to read this board

      Duration, pay, language level and country are read from the advert text by fixed rules, never guessed. "Not stated" means the advert did not say. Needs checking means something is missing: open the advert and confirm it yourself.

      The GJU check compares each internship with the German Year documents. Always confirm with your department before you rely on a posting.

      About the data

      Internships are linked to, not copied: every row points to the original advert. No contact details are published. No cookies and no tracking; this page only remembers your theme and view in your own browser.

      To have an internship removed, contact the maintainer through the repository.
