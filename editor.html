    Skills that no role, project or certificate already covers. The profile combines these with every skill listed on a position, a project or a certification.

From a job ad takes a pasted advert and pulls the skills out of it. The type-ahead offers your own skills first, then ones it has seen in adverts, then the catalogue — and anything you type is accepted whether or not it has heard of it. Duplicates are dropped whatever their capitalisation.

    What happens to this data

    Text is saved in this browser and attachments are stored on this device. This page does not upload your profile or publish it.

    Download .json includes your profile and attachments. Use Import .json to restore a backup here or on another device. The format is compatible with CareerToAI imports.

    Review personal details before sharing an export. Private browsing and clearing site data can remove saved drafts.
